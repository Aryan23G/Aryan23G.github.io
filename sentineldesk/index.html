Project demo · SentinelDesk

An AI assistant for the IT help desk

Every company's IT team gets hundreds of help requests, and some of them are scams pretending to be real requests. SentinelDesk reads each one, checks it for warning signs, decides how urgent it is, and sends it to the right team. Pick a message on the left to watch it work.

STEP 01

A request comes in

Someone emails IT: a forgotten password, a broken printer, or a scam made to look like one of those.

STEP 02

The assistant investigates

It runs five checks, from scanning for scam tricks to looking up help articles, and shows every step.

STEP 03

It decides and routes

It sets the urgency, picks the right team and drafts a reply. Scams go straight to the security team.

Incoming requests

Some are real and some are scams. Can you tell which?

Quick start:

The pretend company's real email domain is company.example.

    Pick a request to begin.
    Phishing

    A fake message designed to trick someone into handing over a password, money or access.

    Priority (P1–P4)

    How urgent a request is. P1 means act now, and P4 means it can wait.

    Security team (SOC)

    The Security Operations Center, the people who investigate and stop attacks.

    Agentic AI

    AI that takes actions with tools, step by step, instead of just answering a question.

    About this demo. This page runs SentinelDesk's built-in rules engine, the same security scanner, sender checks, help articles and routing rules as the full app, directly in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. In the full app, Claude (an AI model) uses these same five tools to make the decision, and the rule-based scanner can overrule the AI if a message tries to trick it.
